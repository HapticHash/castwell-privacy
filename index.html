Castwell Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

In short: Castwell sends your phone's audio straight to the speaker you choose, over your own Wi-Fi. It has no servers, no accounts of its own, no ads and no analytics. What it keeps, it keeps on your phone.

Audio from your phone

To play your phone's sound on a speaker, Castwell uses Android's audio capture, which Android asks you to allow each time with its screen-sharing prompt. Castwell only uses the sound, never the picture on your screen. The audio goes directly from your phone to the AirPlay or Google Cast speaker you picked on your local network. It is not recorded, stored, or sent anywhere else.

Speakers on your network

Castwell looks for speakers on your local network and remembers the ones it finds (their names, models and network addresses), along with any names, order or favourites you set. This stays on your phone and is used only to show and connect to your speakers.

What's playing

If you allow notification access, Castwell reads what is playing on your phone (the title, artist, album artwork, playback position and which app is playing) to show it on the Now Playing screen, offer play controls, and show the track on speakers that support it. Castwell does not read your notifications or messages, and this information is not stored or sent anywhere other than the speaker you are streaming to.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you sign in, Google gives Castwell your name, email address, profile photo and account ID. Castwell keeps these on your phone to show which account you are using, and loads your profile photo from Google. When you buy Castwell Pro while signed in, a one-way hash of your account ID is passed to Google Play with the purchase, so the purchase is tied to your account without telling Google Play who you are. Signing out removes this information from Castwell.

Purchases

Castwell Pro is bought through Google Play, which handles payment. Castwell never sees your payment details; it only learns from Google Play whether you own Pro. Google's Privacy Policy applies to payments.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, it opens an app of your choice, such as your email app, with your message and basic device details (Castwell version, phone model and Android version). You can choose to attach a recent connection log, which records connection events and contains no network addresses. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

Updates

Castwell asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, and reads the list of changes from this website (changelog.json) to show what is new. Nothing about you or your phone is sent with that request beyond what any web request carries, and nothing is stored.

Backups

Android may back up Castwell's settings, and how much of the free listening time you have used, to your Google account under your phone's backup settings, so they come back if you reinstall.

What Castwell does not do

  • It does not sell or share your data.
  • It does not use advertising or analytics services, and it does not track you.
  • It does not run servers of its own or send your data to them.

Children

Castwell is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Everything Castwell keeps is on your phone. Signing out removes your Google account details, and uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes everything else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
